## Delivery notes

- Files are source JPEGs downloaded at a maximum width of 1600px, already compressed by the source delivery URL (234–671KB each). The local toolchain did not provide a working WebP/AVIF encoder, so conversion was not performed solely for format compliance.
- Homepage and interior hero images have explicit width/height metadata, eager loading and high fetch priority. Gallery images are lazy-loaded; their defined grid frames and image dimensions reserve a stable visual area while they load.
- The photos are not distributed as standalone image products. Preserve the above attribution/source record even though Unsplash attribution is not mandatory.
- Before public launch, the customer should approve visual suitability, replace stock imagery with commissioned/consented Happy Hoppers photography where possible, and confirm that the captions/illustrative treatment remain appropriate.
